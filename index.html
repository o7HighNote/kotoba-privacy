Kotoba Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Kotoba ("the app") is a Japanese-language learning app for iOS. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what choices you have. We wrote it to describe exactly what the app does — not a generic template — so it should stay accurate as long as the sections below still match the app's behavior.

The short version

  • Kotoba does not require an account, a sign-up, or a login.
  • Your study progress, saved words, and settings are stored only on your own device. We never see them.
  • Kotoba does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • The only network calls the app makes are: (1) an optional, user-triggered feature to fetch new conversation practice content, and (2) ads served through Google AdMob.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Everything the app tracks — your JLPT level setting, which words you've marked as known or saved, your quiz history, your spaced-repetition review schedule, your study streak, and your app settings (like text-size, romaji, or voice preferences) — is stored locally on your device, in the app's own private storage area. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. If you export a backup file from within the app, that file stays wherever you choose to save it — we never receive a copy.

Optional conversation-generation feature

Kotoba includes an optional feature that lets you request freshly generated conversation-practice content. If you choose to use this feature, the app sends only the following to our backend service: your selected JLPT level, how many conversations to generate, and the titles of conversations you already have (so it can avoid generating duplicates). This request contains no personal information, no device identifier, and no account of any kind — it works the same way for every user. If you never use this feature, the app makes no network request related to it at all, and everything works fully offline.

Advertising

Kotoba is supported in part by advertising, served through Google's AdMob service. When ads are active:

  • Google AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, general device information, and interaction data (such as which ads are shown and tapped) in order to serve and measure ads. Some of this may be used to serve ads tailored to your interests, unless you decline tracking.
  • On iOS, the app will ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before requesting any identifier used for tailored advertising. You can decline this prompt and still use the app and see ads — just not ones tailored to you.
  • You can review Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy and their advertising-specific policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Kotoba offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove ads") that turns off advertising entirely for that device.

In-app purchases

If you buy the "Remove ads" upgrade, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment information — no card numbers, billing address, or Apple ID details reach us. We only learn that the purchase succeeded, so the app can turn ads off on your device.

Children's privacy

Kotoba does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app collects no personal information from any user regardless of age, and stores all progress locally on-device, there is nothing for a child's use of the app to expose beyond what's described above (and, if applicable, the advertising identifiers described in the Advertising section, which follow Apple's and Google's own child-directed-app rules).

Data retention and deletion

Because your study data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it: use the in-app "Reset progress" option to clear it, or delete the app to remove everything at once. We hold no copy to delete on our end, because we never received one.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, there is generally nothing on file for us to show you, correct, or delete on request. If you have questions about this, or believe that's not the case, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes — for example, when advertising is turned on, or if a new feature adds a new kind of network request — we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
